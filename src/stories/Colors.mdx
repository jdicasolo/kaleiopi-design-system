# Colors

Kaleiopi has seven brand colors. Each has a generated 50–950 scale (fixed hue and saturation, HSL lightness interpolation), with the literal brand hex anchored on the step it naturally falls on (outlined below). Every step is a Tailwind utility: `bg-kaleiopi-purple-600`, `text-kaleiopi-navy`, `border-kaleiopi-gold-200`.

Tokens are generated by `scripts/generate-tokens.mjs` into `src/tokens/theme.css`. Edit the script, not the CSS.

| Color | Role | Use for text on white |
|---|---|---|
| Navy `#02154C` | Wordmark, footer, text on accent tints | 500 and darker (400 for large text) |
| Purple `#6245A1` | Primary buttons, links, active states, focus rings | 500 and darker (400 for large text) |
| Teal `#08A8B1` | Decorative and structural accents | 800 and darker |
| Gold `#E6AE49` | Streaks, celebration, donation CTA | 800 and darker (700 for large text) |
| Pink `#CA618E` | Progress bars, step indicators | 600 and darker (500 for large text) |
| Blue `#025298` | Reserved | 700 and darker (500 for large text) |
| White `#FFFFFF` | Primary background | — |

## The accent-badge pattern

Gold, pink and teal fail WCAG AA at their brand hex in both directions. For any badge or callout that carries text, pair the accent's `-50` tint with navy text:

```
bg-kaleiopi-gold-50 text-kaleiopi-navy border border-kaleiopi-gold-200
```

Keep the raw accent hex for non-text fills: progress bars, icons, and large bold headlines.

**Navy caveat:** navy's lighter steps read as a vivid electric blue. For muted navy text use an opacity modifier (`text-kaleiopi-navy/50`) instead of a light step.
